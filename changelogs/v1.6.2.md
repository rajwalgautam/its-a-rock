# v1.6.2

A dedicated home for your beta: the bottom tabs are now **Plans · My Climbing · Settings**, with History a tap away.

## What's new

- **Plans tab** — a new first tab gathers every move plan you've drawn into one gallery, newest first. Tap a plan to open it in the planner. Toggle between **Individual** (one card per plan) and **Stacked** (a climb's plans collapsed into a single stacked card, badged with the count) from the control in the top right; a stack opens the climb so you can pick between its plans.
- **History moved to a button** — the bottom tabs are now Plans, My Climbing, and Settings. History lives on a **History** button on the My Climbing screen, in line with the "This week" heading, and opens the full All-Climbs list with its filters and density control intact.

## Under the hood

- New `getPlans()` query returns one row per plan that has moves, joined to its climb, gym, and photo, newest first; surfaced through `plans`/`loadPlans` on the route store (refreshed on focus and after any route mutation).
- `PlanTile` renders a plan card (photo, grade/gym overlay, a play badge with the move count) and a stacked variant with peeking backing cards and a count badge. The pure `groupPlansByRoute` helper collapses a recency-ordered list into per-climb stacks without reshuffling; unit-tested.
- The Individual/Stacked choice persists as a new `plansLayout` setting (`individual` by default), validated and round-tripped alongside the rest.
- History moves out of the `(tabs)` group to a stacked `app/history.tsx` route (registered on the root stack with a back button); the `(tabs)` layout now lists Plans, My Climbing, and Settings, and My Climbing gains a header History button that pushes it.
