# v1.6.0

See your balance point and watch your beta play itself: the planner gains a green center-of-mass dot and a hands-free playback slideshow.

## What's new

**Route planner**
- **Center of mass** — a green dot now shows your balance point, auto-calculated from wherever your hands and feet currently sit, with dotted lines running out to each limb. It updates live as you drag a marker and travels with each move as you step through the sequence.
- **Adjustable center of mass** — the balance point is a suggestion, not a rule: hold and drag the green dot to place it wherever your body actually is on a move, and it's remembered for that move. Double-tap it to snap back to the auto position. Adjustments are saved with your plan and can be undone like any other edit.
- **Play the route** — a new play button turns the sequence into a hands-free slideshow that steps through your moves on its own; grouped moves land together, just like manual stepping. Choose **Slow**, **Normal**, or **Fast**, and it pauses the moment you scrub, step, or head back to editing.

## Under the hood

- New pure `computeCenterOfMass` (unweighted centroid of the placed limbs) plus `comOverrideAt`/`setCurrentCom`/`clearCurrentCom` helpers in `planSequence`, all unit-tested. The manual override lives on the move that closes each frame; playback reads it per frame, falling back to the centroid.
- Schema **v6**: nullable `com_x`/`com_y` on `plan_moves` (threaded through `PlanMove`/`PlanMoveInput`, `DraftMove`, the query mapper, `savePlanMoves`, and `duplicateNotePlan`). Existing plans migrate cleanly — older moves default to NULL, i.e. a fully auto center of mass.
- The CoM dot renders in `PlanCanvas` from four per-limb live-position shared values that `LimbMarker` mirrors, so the centroid, the dot, and the dashed connector lines are derived on the UI thread — tracking limb drags live and gliding between frames with the same 280ms timing as the markers. The dot has no limb glyph and wears a white ring (and a distinct brighter green, `planCom`) so it never reads as the green left-hand marker.
- Autoplay is a self-re-arming `setTimeout` in the planner screen driven by `PLAYBACK_SPEEDS`; it stops at the last frame and is paused by any manual step/scrub/exit. `PlaybackControls` gains a play/pause button and a Slow/Normal/Fast segmented control. Autoplay and speed are ephemeral screen state (like the current step) — nothing persisted.
