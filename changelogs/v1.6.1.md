# v1.6.1

Play your beta straight from a note, at a new top speed, with your own limb colors — and a smoother balance-point dot along the way.

## What's new

**Route planner**
- **Turbo playback** — the autoplay slideshow gains a fourth speed, **Turbo**, for a quick run-through of the whole sequence. Slow / Normal / Fast are untouched.
- **Play from a note** — tapping a note that has a plan now offers **Play plan**, which opens the planner straight into the autoplaying slideshow. Edit plan and Duplicate plan are still one tap away.
- **Your own limb colors** — pick the marker color for your left and right sides in **Settings → Route planner**. Color is scoped to the side, so the left hand and left foot share one color and the right hand and right foot the other. It defaults to a black left bubble with a white glyph and a white right bubble with a black glyph, and the hand/foot icon always flips to black or white to stay legible on whatever color you choose.

## Fixed

- **Smoother center of mass** — the green balance-point dot no longer stutters during playback when a move brings a new limb onto the wall, or when a frame's center of mass was adjusted by hand. It now glides straight from one frame's balance point to the next.

## Under the hood

- `PLAYBACK_SPEEDS` gains a 400ms **Turbo** entry (still comfortably longer than the 280ms marker glide). The Slow/Normal/Fast/Turbo segmented control and the default-speed index are unchanged otherwise.
- Autoplay-from-note threads a one-shot `autoplay=1` param into `plan/[routeId]`: on load, a plan with moves enters play mode and starts the slideshow, consumed via a ref so re-focusing doesn't restart it. `NoteActionSheet` grows a `Play plan` row (photo notes with a plan) wired through `RouteCard`.
- The CoM stutter is fixed in two places: `LimbMarker` now snaps its live-position mirror on first mount instead of gliding it in from a stale/zero corner (so a limb entering the stance doesn't sweep the connector), and in playback `PlanCanvas` glides the dot to a single per-frame resolved point (`comOverrideAt ?? computeCenterOfMass`) passed down as `comPlayback`, rather than switching between the live centroid and a stale override baseline.
- Limb marker color moves off the theme palette onto two persisted settings, `limbColorLeft`/`limbColorRight` (validated hex, defaulting to `#111111`/`#FFFFFF`). `limbColor(sides, limb)` resolves by `LIMB_SIDE`; a new `contrastColor`/`luminance` util (unit-tested) drives the glyph, dot outline, selector chip, and move-list chip so every swatch stays readable in both themes.
